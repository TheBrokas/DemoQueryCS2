How DemoQueryCS2 works
  1. Sketch a scenario. Pick a map, choose a side (CT/T), and click the radar to place up to five players per side. Smoke and Molly brushes add utility markers that require a smoke or fire burning there. Right-click removes a marker; multi-level maps have an Upper/Lower toggle.
  2. Tune the match. The tolerance slider sets how far real positions may be from your sketch, in game units (0 = the exact zone; ~145u is one smoke radius). Filters narrow by round phase, plant site, economy, the round clock and players alive. With no markers at all, Search finds situations from filters alone — e.g. every 3v2 with under 0:40 on the clock.
  3. Search & watch. Results are real pro rounds ranked by how closely they match. A Scenario stats strip above them shows win rates, win conditions, timing and economy for the matched rounds. Click any result to watch it as a 2D replay — kill feed, bomb location and player view directions included.
